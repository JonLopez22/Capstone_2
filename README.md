# Capstone_2 Project Summary

The notebook walks through the following questions:

Who are the territory managers and what stores are in each territory?
What is the monthly total revenue for each territory over the full data period?
How do stores rank by sales performance within each territory?
Who are the top rewards customers in each territory?
What are the transaction counts and revenue by product category?
What is the recommended marketing focus for next quarter?


Key Findings

New York outperforms Connecticut in total revenue ($4.3M vs $2.4M)
Technology & Accessories is the #1 revenue driver in both territories
Stationery & Supplies has the most transactions but the lowest revenue
Top CT store: New London | Top NY store: New York City
Both territories show a clear upward revenue trend from 2022 to 2025
